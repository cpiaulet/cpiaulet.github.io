Founder & President · 2021–2025

InitiaSciences

In 2021, I founded and led InitiaSciences, an independent one-of-a-kind non-profit in Canada, that opens scientific research to high-school students by giving them the chance to take part in ongoing research projects. The program and workshops offered through InitiaSciences have now been running for 5 years. I led a team of 20+ volunteers through the first five years of this adventure, receiving competitive funding from both government (NovaScience program) and crowdfunding sources. Our flagship research mentoring program is transforming the way young students relate to research by offering funding to encourage participation from underserved youth, and letting them become science ambassadors in their community. I also built longstanding partnerships for InitiaSciences with dozens of private industry partners, research institutes, schools, and colleges.
