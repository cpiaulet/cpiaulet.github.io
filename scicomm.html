Telling the story
of new worlds.

I work with press offices, journalists, and educators to share what telescopes like JWST
are revealing about planets beyond the Solar System, in English and in French.
My team’s work has been featured on TV, radio, video, and print outlets including
The New York Times, Scientific American, NBC News, Sky & Telescope, and Radio-Canada.
