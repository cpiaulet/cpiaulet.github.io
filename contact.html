Bring Exoplanets to Your Audience

I love sharing exoplanet science beyond the research community, and I am happy to give talks to:

  • Teachers and educators, including professional development meetings and workshops
  • Primary to high-school students, in classrooms, clubs, or school events
  • General astronomy enthusiasts, from astronomy clubs to libraries and public events

Talks can be in person or virtual, in English or French. Past audiences include the Chicago/Illinois American Association of Physics Teachers, the American Mensa Annual Gathering, and hundreds of students in Québec classrooms. See Sci Comm & Media and Outreach & Community for more.

Please email me with your audience, preferred dates, and format.
